# Pro Regional Manager — separate from Regional Manager

## Position contracts

**Regional Manager** is an existing Bridger/Hope or Agent/Stability departmental appointment. It does not grant, require, or activate a File Folder. Its applications, team assignments, Lifestyle page, and commission records remain in the `weave_regional_manager_*` system. An ordinary appointment must **never** be inferred from a Pro File Folder or vice versa.

**Pro Regional Manager** is a separate, global company position open to eligible Bridgers, Agents, and Clients. Each approved Pro manager has a verified company appointment, recorded accepted terms, a personally owned Pro File Folder and business plan, independently checked operating country/address, an independently verified Pro activation and department-specific work. **New Agent/Bridger agreements use 20% of verified monthly net business profit instead of company salary**; existing approved salary agreements, including Client Pro staff agreements, remain unchanged. A person retains their original Hope, Stability, or Dream Lovely department.

## Pro is the Folder tier, not the workshop business

Every approved Pro Regional Manager has a **Pro-tier File Folder**. The tier establishes the Folder's grade and visual operating environment, while the person's own life, skills, experience, interests and approved business plan determine the actual business formed inside it. **Pro Regional Manager is an employment position, not a workshop category.** Do not assign every new Pro manager a crypto business, a generic manager workshop, or another person's profession. New Agent/Bridger Pro folders begin in business formation with the applicant's approved business name; the owner builds their own systems and Customer Door within the verified Pro world.

CJ Dorado is the individually owned **Crypto Exchange Workshop** case, not the Pro template. Her existing Client File Number, crypto workshop identity, private exchange journal and Customer Door stay hers alone. Her authorized employees expand her business and its ideals from their assigned role permissions, linking activity and records to the owner Folder and its systems. Other Pro Regional Managers' staff support **their** different businesses; employment titles do not grant employee access to CJ's exchange or alter unrelated business worlds.

Under new Agent/Bridger Pro employment, the owner keeps the independent business and serves WEAVE using it. The company receives a contractual 20% of reconciled monthly net business profit, not gross sales, and does not pay that manager a fixed salary. Legacy signed salary appointments remain separate from File Folder revenue, employee earnings, activation charges and payment evidence. A business plan may describe potential systems without implying that trading, custody or money movement is live.

An approved Pro appointment is not a purchase: neither a payment nor a File Number alone creates a company job, business revenue or salary. A Pro activation fee is never a refundable deposit, a loan repayment, or a precondition for releasing unrelated funds.

## Two official Pro Regional Manager entrances

**Agent and Bridger — Lifestyle position application.** An existing authenticated Agent/Stability or Bridger/Hope opens `/weave/lifestyles/pro-regional-manager` from the Pro Regional Manager position in Lifestyle. The application records operating country/region/city/address, Pro business name, a four-part business plan and departmental daily work plan. Administration reviews the private application at `/admin/pro-regional-manager-applications`, independently verifies the 20% net-profit employment terms, operating continent and precise regional fee quote, and records approval as **approved pending Pro verification**. This is not a File Folder activation or payment instruction. The draft implementation now reserves an original-role Pro File Number after Administration approval, collects signed terms and blockchain payment evidence, and requires an independently verified **quoted** TRC-20 payment before activating the owner's Folder and daily work: Africa is greater than zero and below 60 USDT at an Administrations-set quote; Europe is 680 USDT. Other continents have no approved quote yet. Existing legacy salary appointments retain their prior 680 USDT activation contract. No applicant should be instructed to pay until the official wallet is shown in their verified activation workspace.

**External Client — issued File Number verification.** An invited Client outside the existing WEAVE Agent/Bridger Lifestyle enters `/client/pro-verification`, presents an issued File Number, submits their business plan, signature and actual location/address, and receives private session-bound verification status. Administration reviews identity, company-approved Pro staff offer and terms at `/admin/pro-file-number-verifications` before the approved activation payment; account ownership and the File Folder are verified before starting operations. CJ Dorado is the first Dream Lovely/Client Pro Regional Manager test.

Both entrances belong to the same **Pro Regional Manager** position, with role-specific daily work and a Pro File Folder; they are not the ordinary Regional Manager application path. Never expose private Client identity or salary to an applicant through File Number lookup alone.

## Pro onboarding and verification

1. Administration creates or verifies a **Pro Regional Manager** appointment under the person's original source department, recording their name, personally owned enterprise, accepted compensation model and agreement reference, verified operating continent, quoted fee, File Number and intended territory. Client legacy salary appointments retain their separate salary records.
2. The recipient opens a verification workspace with the issued File Number, not a password; submits their File Folder business name, business plan, actual country, region, city, address, e-signature and accepted terms.
3. Administration verifies the recipient independently and checks the approved Pro appointment, regional fee and accepted compensation agreement *before* presenting payment instructions.
4. The recipient submits evidence of the approved Pro activation fee (current CJ implementation: **680 USDT on TRON/TRC-20**). Administration independently checks actual receipt, matching address/network/amount and unique transaction before activating; avoid charging for the same event twice.
5. Once identity/account ownership, original department, company appointment, Pro Folder ownership and activation are all confirmed, the account receives the **Pro Regional Manager** environment. Its visible palette follows the Pro File Folder; its operations follow the department. File Number alone is never an authentication credential.
6. No ordinary Regional Manager appointment is created and no ordinary manager Lifestyle pay is inferred from this process.

## International daily workspace standard

- **My work today:** today's authorized activities, next actions and status of completion
- **My business:** personal Pro File Folder, constructed systems, Customer Door and business plan milestones
- **My territory:** approved country/region, regional market relationships and business expansion
- **My company position:** the applicable signed 20% net-profit partnership (or historical signed salary), notices and authorized company assignments
- **My records:** verified orders, receipts, real operating costs, monthly net profit, company 20% share where applicable, separate historic salary, reconciliations and compliance issues
- **My reports:** daily completed work, obstacles, next actions and Administration responses

Use the actual source department to select the daily functions. No title grants cross-department permissions.

## New original-role Pro participation — 20% net business profit

Effective for **new** Agent/Bridger Pro agreements accepted under the updated versioned terms. WEAVE provides structured business support and authorized company opportunities; the manager performs approved company work through their own Pro File Folder business, retaining business ownership. The manager does not receive a new fixed monthly WEAVE salary. The manager contributes 20% of independently verified **monthly net business profit** to WEAVE, with a zero contribution for a verified break-even or loss month. Monthly assessment must reconcile funded receipts, worker pay, refunds, other expenses, tax and external revenue before collection; current source does not yet provide a verified profit-share invoice/payout automation. Do not automatically debit an owner's wallet or treat self-reported costs as independently verified. Existing Client/Dream Lovely salary appointments and previously signed Agent/Bridger salary terms require separate contractual consent before any change.
