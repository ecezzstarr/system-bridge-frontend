# System Switch — WEAVE platform currency and future digital-asset roadmap

## Current status

**System Switch** is the public name of WEAVE's existing internal platform currency, formerly displayed as Flame Coin. This is a naming change, not the issuance or registration of a cryptocurrency.

- All existing balances, authorized charges, pricing calculations, commissions and transaction histories retain their numeric values.
- Historical records and database identifiers, including `FLAME_COIN`, `balance_trx`, `amount_flame_coin`, and existing URL/API paths, remain in place until a separately authorized, tested accounting migration.
- User-facing historical records map legacy currency labels to **System Switch** without changing the stored financial evidence.
- The existing TRX reference and conversion settings remain unchanged by the naming release.
- The System Switch internal balance and the System Switch gateway to the 3D world are distinct capabilities that share the company name.

## Future digital cryptoasset proposal — not implemented

WEAVE may separately evaluate a digital token named System Switch. Before any launch or public cryptoasset claim, it would need to:

1. Verify name rights, legal entity structure, applicable rules, registrations and licences in every relevant jurisdiction.
2. Define supply, issuance, reserve backing if any, redemption terms, consumer disclosures, custody, governance and how existing accounts would or would not migrate.
3. Complete blockchain/contract architecture, independent code and financial-security audits, and custody/key-management controls.
4. Establish independent reconciliation between existing company platform balances and any proposed on-chain units; avoid silent conversions or duplicate credit.
5. Secure required approvals, publish terms and risk disclosures, and authorize any migration only after operational testing and clear user communication.

**Do not describe System Switch as an already-registered cryptocurrency, a tradable on-chain coin, a stablecoin, or an investment with guaranteed returns.**
