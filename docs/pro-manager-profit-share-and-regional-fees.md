# Agent and Bridger Pro Regional Manager — owner business and company partnership

## Scope and ownership
Eligible Agents (Stability) and Bridgers (Hope) can apply for an Administrations-approved **Pro Regional Manager** position. They retain their original department, own an individual Pro File Folder, choose their lawful business through an approved plan, operate that business and serve the growth of WEAVE through authorized company work. Their File Folder is not company property; neither an activation fee nor a title grants privileges to another Client's business.

This **new Agent/Bridger agreement** is different from existing **Client/Dream Lovely Pro staff employment**, including already signed monthly salary offers. Ordinary Regional Manager appointments remain separate. Existing approved salary agreements cannot be silently replaced without new voluntary consent.

## New terms
- New Agent/Bridger Pro agreements have **no fixed monthly WEAVE salary**. The owner contributes **20% of independently verified monthly net business profit** to WEAVE; it is not 20% of turnover, gross receipts, File Folder value or customer deposits.
- A month with zero or negative verified profit has **zero profit-share charge**. Profit requires reconciliation of customer receipts, refunds, payroll/Legion compensation, permitted operating costs, taxes and external business channels in consistent currency and periods.
- A published Customer Door order or paid status is not automatically a settled receipt; verified settlements and external provider evidence matter.
- Existing code has funded Customer Door settlements and owner-reported expenses. **Owner-reported costs are not independently approved costs**. The present implementation must not issue an enforceable debit or label estimated profit-share as paid. A separately reviewed monthly reporting, assessment, invoice, payment and dispute path is required before collecting the 20% by automation.

## One-time Pro File Folder activation quote
- **Africa**: greater than 0 and strictly **below 60 USDT**. No exact Africa fee was specified by company instruction. Administrations must enter and approve the individual quoted amount before the applicant may sign or receive payment instructions. The system must not invent a default fee.
- **Europe**: **680 USDT**.
- **Other continents**: no new quote or payment is authorized until a separately published policy exists.
- Continent depends on independently checked operating location (actual country and address), not IP or self-declared eligibility. Administrations records the verified continent, fee and reference on the applicant's approval. The applicant sees the precise fee and signs that quote. Forensics independently checks the exact on-chain amount and company destination before activation.
- Existing signed/approved legacy Agent/Bridger salary appointments retain their existing 680 USDT contractual activation quote. Existing Client Pro staff verification and agreed salary are unchanged.

## State and accounting requirements
New applications: submitted business plan and regional work plan → Administration verifies eligibility, location, continent, individual fee quote and 20% agreement reference → Pro File Number reserved → candidate reviews and signs the profit-share terms and exact activation quote → verified payment → private Pro Folder and original-department workspace. No payment itself authorizes employment.

Company participation has two distinct flows: real company work/partnership responsibilities and separately accounted net-profit participation. No automatic payroll in the new contract. Implement monthly net-profit source evidence, independent cost review, payout ledger/receipt, adjustments/refunds, dispute procedure, currency conversion and multi-channel reconciliation before automatic 20% remittances. Historical receipts and agreements remain intact.
