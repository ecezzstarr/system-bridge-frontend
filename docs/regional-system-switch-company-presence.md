# Regional System Switch Gates — Company of Time

Each active **ordinary Regional Manager** is responsible for an authorized regional System Switch gate of daily participation. The gate is not a second global website or a privately owned File Folder. The world reached through System Switch remains the one shared 3D open world.

## Institutional movement

- Administrations approves a Regional Manager (Bridger or Agent) and assigns ordinary Bridgers, ordinary Agents and verified Distribution/Food Distribution lead managers. Their assistants inherit their approved leads.
- The Manager's approved application defines the region's **operating label**. Membership comes from Administration assignment, not automatic GPS location, IP-address assumptions, or public browsing.
- The gate shows the appointed manager's latest authorized direction and aggregated signs of actual company activity: today's worker check-ins, completed tasks, completed prospect purchases, paid and fulfilled food orders, and confirmed Client File Folder crossings.
- Staff can see evidence that other people are participating through the company while retaining privacy: no Client names, payment amounts, private check-in summaries, phone numbers, customer addresses, prospect details or private File Folder access.
- Only the appointed ordinary Regional Manager publishes a direction for staff (up to three per regional day); the verified staff receive a generic notification directing them to the gate.
- A Bridger or Agent continues in their original department and accesses the regional gate through verified assignment alone. Buying Agentic-Bridger or owning a File Folder is not a condition of regional participation.
- The gate is visible before the shared System Switch crossing, on both Hope and Stability daily dashboards, and inside the regional workspaces. It refreshes every 20 seconds without faking missing events.
- The Regional Manager still verifies assigned work, supports staff, coordinates leaders and their assistants, and escalates exceptions. Administrations sees the approved region and latest direction in Regional Workforce Control.

## Distinctions and safeguards

Regional company signals are not a map of physically nearby people, proof of wages, a payment ledger, or an indication that every worker is active. A region is currently an Administration-approved operating assignment, not a geofence.

Activity counts are never interpreted as company earnings. Staff tasks and well-being are handled in authenticated workspaces; sales/payment verification is reserved to its owning company systems. Absent evidence is marked unavailable, not zero.

The **System Switch** gateway and **System Switch** platform currency share a name but are separate functions. The future digital cryptocurrency project is not part of regional gate operations.
