# WEAVE Image House · offline business formation and material branding (10 November 2026)

## Company's operating direction

The Image House is no longer a standalone sample maker. Hope (Bridgers), Stability (Agents), and Dream Lovely (Clients) can use it to enter real customer design businesses through a six-step progression:
1. Choose one service category and save a real offer, audience, indicative customer quote and manageable daily outreach goal.
2. Produce an example with company original vector artwork; clothing, product packaging, merchandise and artist editions have printable-zone mockup previews. A preview does not represent an actual manufactured product.
3. Open a producer's public image service door by saving their existing Image House service profile. Actual customers enter requests through that door, not through invented leads.
4. Create a ready-to-share advertisement with a real service URL, then deliberately distribute it using an approved channel. The system does not post to social accounts or claim measured reach. A self-reported share is not an advertising platform confirmation.
5. Review accepted customer briefs, agree scope and price, pay the displayed System Switch production-license fee and deliver the licensed asset. A separate customer commission is a separate license. External customer cash remains reported/unverified until independently confirmed.
6. Use Distribution Studio, Video Ad Studio, Manager Support (for Agent and Bridger) and existing File Folder systems to expand repeatable operations. Do not promise income, customer acquisition, outside-stock printing or free funding.

## Company workshops and routes

- /image-workshop — original paid commercial template catalogue, company receipts, public image Service Door, customer job queue.
- /image-workshop/branding — offline material mockup editor. Ten physical/creative presentations support own PNG/JPEG/WEBP artwork (up to 5MB), label text, artwork placement, size, rotation and color. Canvas keeps uploaded artwork in the participant's device; the SHA-256 digest, text and positioning are included in the company license fingerprint. Final locally composed PNG requires a successful private paid license. It is a commercial design proof, not a direct-to-fabric manufacturing or print file.
- /image-workshop/business — participant business launcher, six services, six guided action steps, predesigned ad previews, saved launch plan, ad drafts, manual sharing evidence, customer door and licensed work counters.
- /admin/image-branding — Administrations catalogue curation (featured flags), active role startup counts, campaign counts, actual System Switch license earnings, service kits and operating guides. The existing Image House fee control remains within /image-workshop for Administrations.
- /image-workshop/ai — historical gallery only for users who already have prior generated assets. Owner-scoped private downloads are preserved. Image generation POST responds with 410 and never contacts OpenAI or other paid provider. Host API keys and stale flags cannot re-enable paid generation.

## Business template catalogue

The original 36 vector categories remain. Eleven additional original materials and artist-oriented templates are added, yielding 47 templates total. New scenes include shirt, hoodie, tote, cap, mug, retail box, phone case, release-record presentation and gallery canvas. These are real editable vector scenes with tailored text/colour palettes, not remotely generated photorealistic background replacement. Image mockups advertise a concept and must not be used to claim physical inventory exists.

The companion high-fidelity visual reference images made during design discussion are not interchangeable with editable product layers: photographic text printed inside a picture cannot be programmatically rewritten. Only actual editable vector/text/artwork layers are offered as live template controls.

## Economics and records

- The company's existing image_sample license checkout verifies the user, locks the actual primary wallet, debits only the published approved System Switch fee, issues a receipt and records the licensed production. Retrying the same design/accepted customer commission is idempotent.
- A new uploaded artwork's SHA-256 digest and placement/text properties form part of its production fingerprint, so visibly different customer outputs can be recorded separately. Uploaded media bytes stay local and are **not** persisted in the company's database; the user must keep their own original.
- The new startup tables store a participant's current branding plan, independently keyed ad drafts and a transparent self-reported external-share status. Twelve new ad preparations per account per rolling 24h, with a per-user transaction lock to prevent concurrent bypass and an idempotent client key.
- Administrations can feature existing originals; this does not overwrite or delete historical licensed work. All roles are reverified on every company API.
- Prepared ads, reported shares, Service Door requests, and actual company receipts remain distinct. No external customer payment, ad reach, printing revenue or profit is inferred from marketing clicks or self-reported activity.

## Real photographic product materials (10 November 2026)

The existing 47 vector templates remain openly labelled illustrations. They are not presented as literal photographs of products. Physical apparel and merchandise material templates now use credited photographs of actual physical items from Unsplash pages or existing WEAVE product photography, and these photo-backed examples appear in the catalogue, Ads and Administrations.

The editor uses a real photographed base or accepts a participant's own actual product photo. It applies logos, artwork, type, size, position and rotation using an offline local canvas. Customer product photos stay on the device. Each photograph digest and edited placement become part of the existing company production-license fingerprint.

A remote image is not trusted until it can be loaded and read from the export canvas. If unavailable, export is blocked before checkout and the participant can provide their own photograph. License/receipt authorization remains required for high-resolution commercial delivery.

Sources: free Unsplash photographs under Unsplash License with source credits, and actual WEAVE owned product photography. Brand or identifiable-person rights may require further permission. Stock-photo display does not represent WEAVE physical stock. WEAVE does not sell an unmodified original photo as-is. Photographic previews and product printing are distinct deliverables. Remote photos need network and CORS; local customer uploads are supported without AI API.

Validation: tests/real-photo-premium-fidelity.cjs, Image House licensing regressions, TypeScript, full production build and mobile upload/export acceptance.
