# Flame Presence — Client world expansion contract

## Institutional truth
The Client remains the owner and source of direction. The Lord or Lady is not required to personally operate every business task. Administration, Forensics, Mandate, Attorney, Bridgers and Legions carry their assigned responsibilities. AI Presence participates within those boundaries, not above them.

## Current implementation versus intended capability
- AI File Folder identities and product/build actions already exist in the repository.
- Identity, a Customer Door label and a published product do **not** establish external publishing, prospect conversations, sales or recurring AI execution.
- Ember Works is an initial Flame AI identity; do not label it as a live autonomous sales agent until the actual connection, authorization and executor are verified.
- The TypeScript contract in `lib/flame-presence-expansion.ts` models authority, connections, movements and readiness. It is not itself a scheduler, API integration, database migration or live agent.

## Required movement
1. Administration approves a scoped, expiring mandate for an AI identity and a Client File Folder.
2. Mandate validates countries, products, budget, connections and authorized actions.
3. Each provider connection is verified; secrets stay server-side and are never exposed to the File Folder browser.
4. A durable job queue and scheduled worker claim work, retry transient failures and record immutable provider references.
5. Research and creative output become proposals. Publication requires Client authorization and platform permissions.
6. Incoming opt-in enquiries are attributed to the correct Customer Door, with human Bridger/Legion handoff when needed.
7. Forensics independently verifies orders, payments and receipts; an AI claim is not a settled sale.
8. The Client sees verified progress, unresolved work and exceptions within their world.

## Non-negotiable acceptance checks
- Restarting the worker does not erase unfinished work or duplicate publication.
- Disconnected accounts cannot be represented as live.
- A revoked/expired mandate stops new external actions.
- No AI can approve its own settlement, impersonate a person or promise a sale.
- Each activity is attributable to the AI identity, Client, position, provider and order where applicable.
- Mobile File Folder shows honest states: identity, awaiting mandate, connections needed, ready, active, paused.

This foundation precedes any claim of globally autonomous expansion.
