# The Unbuilt Door · Bridger-owned verified demand outreach

## Principle
File Folders are sold through Hope Bridgers' activities. The Unbuilt Door is **not** a separate File Folder storefront, transaction path or company-led direct sales bypass. It supplies real, independently verified market demand to Bridgers, who accompany the human crossing, the sale, and Client continuity.

## Routes
- `/unbuilt-door`: public read-only verified opportunity board plus private customer-request intake
- `/bridger/unbuilt-door`: Bridger outreach workshop; selects a real active Bridge AI from the logged-in Bridger's own paths, then shares that Bridge AI's own canonical `/bridge/[code]` link (optionally with a verified need parameter)
- `/admin/unbuilt-door`: Administrations confirmation of authentic demand and view of proposals grouped by responsible Bridger
- `/bridge/[code]`: existing official Bridger Bridge AI path for conversation, File Folder payment crossing, confirmation and continuity

## Work cycle
1. A requester voluntarily states a need. Until verified independently by Administrations, it remains private; no invented opportunities are published.
2. Administrations verifies a real request and records the basis. A redacted, non-identifying summary appears publicly. This is a confirmed **inquiry**, not an order.
3. A Bridger chooses an opportunity and an **already-issued active Bridge AI path** they own. In their personal Bridge AI workshop they publish their specialization, business purpose, distinctive expression and welcome. Those settings are private to the Bridger's editing authority, but public in the owner's Bridge experience.
4. The outward invitation links to **`/bridge/[code]`**, optionally `?need=[verified-demand-id]`. The prospect first encounters the Bridger's personal Weave intelligence, backed by the original Bridge AI conversation and File Folder sales crossing. The prospect can choose to inspect verified requests inside the companion Unbuilt Door (`/unbuilt-door?bridge=[code]`) and submit a private business response.
5. The Unbuilt Door server writes the response with **bridger_id and bridge_code from the trusted database**, never from an unverified user-supplied Bridger ID. Business responses cannot be submitted without a valid Bridge.
6. The prospect continues into `/bridge/[code]`. The **Bridger** explains File Folder value, conducts the sale through the existing official path, and accompanies the Client crossing.
7. Administrations independently verifies payment, issues the File Number, and releases Client activation. Only the original verified sale/commission ledger may recognize company earnings or Bridger commission; an outreach response creates neither.
8. The Bridger maintains authorized Client continuity after activation. Administrations closes old needs.


## Personal Weave Intelligence · force of expression
- `/bridger/bridge-ai` now contains **My Weave Intelligence**: an editable specialization, purpose, public title, individual expression, and introductory welcome for each active owned Bridge AI path.
- `/api/bridger/bridge-expression` reads and updates only the authenticated, active Bridger's own paths; an individual's profile cannot change Weave authority, verification, official pricing, or the company ledger.
- `/bridge/[code]` displays the personal expression, the verified opportunity when supplied, the active Bridge AI chat entrance, and the existing File Folder purchase crossing.
- The Bridge AI conversation receives the specialization as bounded, non-authoritative context alongside the core WEAVE mandate. The original Bridge AI engine remains in operation; the personal expression is a distinct practical lens, **not** an unrestricted new agent or a claim of sentience.
- The workshop is the instrument. Its expression becomes meaningful when an individual user's words are recognized and carried into a verified next action. The Bridger remains a distinct person with responsibility for the relationship.

## Guardrails
- No direct File Folder purchase link or generic checkout inside the Unbuilt Door funnel.
- No fabricated buyers, paid orders, price promises or investment-return claims.
- No anonymous outbound messaging. Bridgers only copy invitations to people who agree to receive them.
- A typed code never changes owner attribution. The backend checks `bridge_ais` and `users` to establish real Bridger ownership.
- Private requester and prospect contact details stay inside the admin-authenticated review register; public listings redact email/phone/links. Administrations must still inspect each record for inadvertent personal identifiers.
- Existing File Folder tiers, pricing, commission calculations, and authorized payment verification are unchanged.
- This pilot does not automatically match proposals to paid sales for a sales dashboard. Conversion must use verified Bridge-linked payments from existing company ledgers.
- Add platform-level bot protection and audit/reporting before scaling this public intake.

## Hypothetical example
A real customer requests recurring office pastry delivery. Administrations speaks to the requester and independently confirms the inquiry. A Hope Bridger recognizes an interested bakery entrepreneur, asks permission to share **the Bridger's own personal Bridge AI link**, and lets the entrepreneur explore that verified opportunity through the Bridger's specialized Weave intelligence before discussing whether a File Folder operating environment is useful. The entrepreneur's proposal is recorded against the verified Bridger; the sale, if elected, takes place only through that Bridger's actual Bridge. No income, customer assignment or payment is presumed.

## Pilot metrics
Count genuine submitted customer needs; administrator-verified needs; Bridger-generated invitations and manual consented contacts; private business responses per Bridge; and independently verified File Folder purchases matched through the existing sales and commissions ledger. Do not conflate offers and actual paid orders.
